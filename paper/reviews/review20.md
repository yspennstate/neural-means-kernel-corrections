# Independent publication review 20

**Vote on publishing the submitted version unchanged: NO.**

**Scope.** I read the full extracted original main manuscript, `tmp/pdfs/original.txt`, and inspected the matching original supplementary statements, proofs, experiment descriptions, and implementation disclosures in `source_repo/paper`. I did not consult the other reviewers or edit the manuscript. This is a review of the submitted version, not a vote on an unseen revision. I did not rerun the expensive experiments or independently authenticate all external references.

The paper contains a plausible publishable empirical study: its controlled frozen-feature comparison, seed replication, and finite-pool residual analysis are useful. Its principal ingredients are correctly identified as established constructions. My negative vote concerns remaining mathematical edge cases, contradictions between the headline interpretation and actual tables, and a disclosed implementation defect whose downstream rerun is absent. Those issues are repairable, but disclosure and corrected numbers must not be confused.

## Findings that affect publication readiness

1. **The structural-pipeline results have a known, unrecomputed out-of-fold defect.** Supplement S9 (`impl.tex`, paragraph “Cross-fitted labels and out-of-fold centering”) says the out-of-fold kernel predictions centered targets by their pooled mean over all 19000 rows, allowing held-out-fold targets into the centering; it also says the script was repaired but the downstream refiner and pipeline reruns are queued and absent from this version. In contrast, main Section 3.1 says the kernel channel “never sees its own target.” That assertion is false for the reported runs. The numerical impact cannot be established from the statement that the effect is small or of order 1/sqrt(n), particularly when several claimed gains are hundredths of a percentage point. Identify the affected rows prominently, remove the absolute no-leakage assertion, and either replace them with verified corrected reruns or explicitly restrict their role to exploratory results from the disclosed original implementation. Do not present them as verified clean-pipeline performance.

2. **Several headline OCO-2 claims contradict the paper's own scores.** Table 1 calls the two OCO-2 criteria “both of that paper's error metrics,” while Section 5 says both are defined here on the released reduced representation and neither is the source paper's acceptance test. The latter scope is the one that should govern the table caption. The introduction describes improvement on both metrics at ten seeds per band, but Section 5 and Supplement S7 report only nine of ten WCO2 seeds on radiance. State the exact counts. At the end of Section 6.2, the combination is said to improve on “every single model on both metrics at once,” yet Table 6 gives reduced error 4.12% for the combination versus 4.11% for the flat-feature Matérn head. The supportable statement is improvement over the rescored external emulator in mean on both criteria, with the documented per-seed exception; it is not simultaneous domination of every component model.

3. **A small but real theorem edge-case failure remains.** Proposition 6.7 permits correlation rho=-1 through its sole condition rho<min(t,1/t), but states partial V/partial t>0. At rho=-1, V=0 for every t>0 and the displayed derivative is zero. Require -1<rho<min(t,1/t) for the strict derivative conclusions, or state the non-strict boundary case separately. Proposition 6.1(i) should also explicitly require 0<e1<=e2 when defining rho=S12/(e1e2); if one member has zero risk, no strict improvement is possible and the ratio formulation is unnecessary. These are local repairs, not failures of the second-moment method.

4. **The formal guarantees and the implemented predictor are not consistently separated.** Section 6.9's caveat correctly says the correction is fitted on cross-fitted residual labels and deployed over the full-data mean. Elsewhere, the introduction, uncertainty discussion, and conclusion say the kernel correction “brings” the stated residual bound without repeating the mismatch. For a clean formulation, define the actually deployed mean m_full, define D=m_full(X)-m_cf(X), and state the pathwise bound with the additional term ||k(u,X)(K+n lambda I)^(-1)D||. No extrapolated out-of-fold function is required. The unmeasured term must remain unmeasured. Likewise, Section 6.1 properly calls validation bounds conditional capacity calculations for fixed candidates, but Section 3.2 says stacking is “statistically almost free,” and Section 6.2 says Proposition S1.3 establishes fitting is safe. Those sound like guarantees for the deployed adaptive procedure, which Section 6.1 explicitly denies. Qualify or remove them.

5. **The coverage statement loses conditions when summarized in the main text.** Proposition S3.1 gives the upper coverage bound only when scores are almost surely distinct; Section 6.5 displays it unconditionally. The Beta and Beta-binomial statements need conditionally iid continuous scores, which is stronger than bare exchangeability. Also define the order statistic when ceil((1-alpha)(m+1))=m+1, normally by appending +infinity, and distinguish this endpoint from the finite Beta law. Randomly dividing an already inspected test block does not by itself establish that every study-level model decision was independent of the calibration values. Supplement S9 acknowledges repeated use of both public test blocks, so main-text language about exact independence should be restricted to the fixed fitted predictor and stated sampling assumptions, not elevated to a statement about untouched confirmatory evaluation.

6. **The interpretation of kernel representations is too categorical.** Section 6.3 says a stationary kernel “reaches only its native space,” and Section 4.5 says the residual is not a function of the load in any way a Matérn RKHS on R41 can see. The displayed native-space theorem requires RKHS membership; it does not prove approximation impossibility outside that space, or exclude a better input-space kernel construction. The relevant empirical finding is the behavior of the evaluated kernel family at the sampled budgets and hyperparameters. Moreover, Table 6 says every feature head chose a search boundary and the wider-grid experiment is unfinished, so the representation ladder is controlled for this budget and grid, not an unrestricted best-possible comparison.

7. **The anisotropic “informal proposition” should not retain unsupported theorem-like guarantees.** Supplement S2.5 is responsibly called informal, but still claims a high-probability rate for validation-optimal ridge regression. Its derivation treats approximate rank as exact rank at working resolution, changes Sobolev-order conventions, and says validation cost is lower order without supplying the relationship between validation size, n, smoothness and dimension that would imply this. It is better retained as a heuristic discussion with its idealization and empirical failure of the rate interpretation; it should not read as an established convergence theorem. This is not essential to the main empirical results and can be repaired without replacing those results.

## What checks out and can be retained

- The squared-risk identity, equicorrelation formula, entrywise convex lower bound, upper bracket, positive-definite signed-weight solution, and architecture/seed block lower bound follow by the displayed elementary quadratic-form arguments. The sixty-member floor is a bound for that measured pool in the RMS metric, not a universal data floor, and Section 4.6 generally makes this restriction clearly.
- The regularized error representer gives the exact worst-case factor P-tilde. The minimax interpolation argument and the nugget identity in Theorem 6.10 are sound at the stated positive-definite design and fixed native-space ball. They are classical recovery facts with a useful residual reading, not a new unconditional neural-operator guarantee. In the equality construction in the supplementary proof of Theorem 6.9, the line r1(X)=g_u(X) omits the scaling factor used to define r1; correcting this line preserves the proof.
- Table 5 correctly separates mean and RMS scores. It explicitly identifies the hindsight optimizer as optimizing RMS and not the mean, so its 4.581% mean value should not be described elsewhere as the best possible mean-error convex combination.
- The constant-width conformal comparison is a useful negative result: the power-function score need not rank pointwise errors well even when conformal calibration supplies marginal coverage.
- The paper is appropriately cautious in several places about the absent refined-solver experiment. Keep the finite-pool shared-residual finding, and remove categorical statements that its persistence establishes the final few learnable hundredths or a numerical-data floor.

## Supplement footnote and proof pointers required by the user

The first-page GitHub footnote currently lists code, data pointers and per-run summaries but does not explicitly direct readers to the supplement. The later Supplementary material section does provide a link, which is useful but does not satisfy the requested footnote change. Add “The online supplement, including detailed proofs and additional experiments, is available there as `paper/supplement.pdf`,” preferably with a direct hyperlink.

A global sentence “Proofs are collected in Supplement S8” is not a local pointer for every result. Add a precise proof location immediately after each externally proved theorem, proposition, corollary and lemma, in both documents. For the original numbering the main results map as follows:

## Recommendation

Revise as a careful empirical comparison and diagnostics paper with a supporting mathematical toolkit. Existing evidence can support that kind of contribution, but it does not support all of the current headline wording. Local mathematical and editorial corrections should be applied directly. The known fold-centering defect must be distinguished from those textual repairs: corrected experimental performance requires actual corrected reruns. Subject to that distinction and appropriate positioning, I would be willing to review a revised submission; I cannot guarantee acceptance at a particular journal, and I do not cast a YES vote on results I have not seen.
