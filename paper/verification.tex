Every result stated in Section~\ref{sec:theory} is checked numerically, and
the checks are released with the code. They come in two kinds. The first kind
builds a synthetic object whose ground truth is known exactly and tests the
stated inequality or identity on it: the certified bound of
Theorem~\ref{thm:or} is evaluated on a residual constructed inside the native
space with exactly known norm, at several nuggets, where any pointwise
violation beyond numerical precision would falsify the statement; the
leave-half-out identity and unbiasedness of Proposition~\ref{prop:kf} are
checked by direct enumeration and Monte Carlo; the effective-dimension
identity of Lemma~\ref{lem:deff} is checked to machine precision and the
closed form of Corollary~\ref{cor:mp} against the empirical spectrum of an
i.i.d.-entry Gram matrix; and the rate mechanism of
Proposition~\ref{prop:aniso} is exercised on a controlled warped target
$G=g(A\,\cdot)$ with a known singular-value profile, where the adapted metric
must dominate at every sample size and improve the empirical rate. The second
kind evaluates the statements whose subject is the pipeline itself on the
pipeline's own released artifacts, once per seed: the symmetrization
inequality of Proposition~\ref{prop:sym} on every trained member (reflection
averaging must not increase test error); the second-moment identity of
Proposition~\ref{prop:secmom} on the measured residual matrix; the bracket of
Corollaries~\ref{cor:floor} and~\ref{cor:floorpinch} against the realized
convex stack; the selection conditions of
Propositions~\ref{prop:percoord}, \ref{prop:selectcoord}
and~\ref{prop:affine} against the realized selections, with the measured
constants $b$, $W$ and $\kappa$ of Corollary~\ref{cor:certificate} and the
certificate evaluated next to the realized excess; the prospective
scoreboard of the two-member rule of Proposition~\ref{prop:secmom}(i) across
all member pairs, seeds and problems; the coverage of
Proposition~\ref{prop:conf} across seeds, where each seed's observed value is
placed against the exact Beta--Binomial law of Remark~\ref{rem:covlaw} and
the count inside its central band is reported; and the data-scaling sweep of
the two raw-input kernel rows, refit at nested training sizes, where
Proposition~\ref{prop:aniso} requires the empirical exponents to coincide at
the measured near-full approximate rank, in contrast to the synthetic
rank-gap target on which the adapted exponent must improve. The bound
comparisons are reported as measured constants, bound value and realized
excess side by side; where an oracle risk is needed it is estimated by a
deterministic refit on the test arrays, which can only make the reported
ratios conservative. The synthetic checks report pass or fail against
explicit tolerances; the artifact checks report the measured quantities
themselves.
